# stock'd
a clean solution for your home pantry inventory management problems! never throw out old groceries you forgot about in the back of your fridge again.
## Features
- **Receipt Scanning** - drag and drop or upload a photo of a receipt to automatically extract food and drink items
- **Expiration Date Estimation** each item comes with a realistic estimated shelf life (e.g., "7 days", "1 month").
- **Smart Storage Sorting** — items are automatically tagged as **Pantry**, **Fridge**, or **Freezer** based on what they are.

## Stack
- **Frontend:** React (Vite)
- **AI:** Google Generative AI SDK (`@google/generative-ai`) — Gemini 2.5 Flash
